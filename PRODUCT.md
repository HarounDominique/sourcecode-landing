# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Senior backend and platform engineers working in large Java/Spring (and Spring Boot) codebases, plus the AI coding agents they drive. They reach the page from a README, an HN/Reddit link, or a colleague — skeptical, technical, allergic to marketing. Their context: an enterprise monorepo where a one-line change can ripple across dozens of modules, and an agent that keeps re-parsing the repo on every session. The job: decide in under a minute whether `sourcecode` is real engineering or vapor, then `pip install` and try it.

## Product Purpose

`sourcecode` builds a deterministic semantic model of a Java/Spring repository — locally, statically, no API keys. It traces systemic blast radius with security enrichment, scores migration readiness (e.g. javax→jakarta), audits Spring TX/security anomalies, maps event topology, and exports C4 architecture. A persistent structural cache means cold-scan once, sub-second on every later agent session. Success = a skeptical engineer trusts the output enough to install, and an agent gets persistent structural context instead of re-parsing.

## Brand Personality

Precise, grounded, quietly confident. Three words: **deterministic, considered, honest.** It speaks like good engineering documentation, not like a SaaS funnel — lowercase wordmark, exact numbers, real CLI output shown rather than claimed. The emotional goal is *trust through evidence*: the warmth says "made by people who think carefully"; the terminal says "and who ship real tools."

## Anti-references

- **Its own previous skin**: neon-cyan-on-pure-black cyber-terminal. That is the category's first-order reflex for "Java dev tool" and reads as generic.
- **SaaS dev-tool landing pages**: gradient-text headings, hero-metric template, identical icon-card grids, purple/blue glow, "10x your workflow" copy.
- **The pure editorial-magazine clone** (display-serif italic + ruled three-column + uppercase mono kickers + zero imagery). We borrow Anthropic's *warmth and intellectual calm*, not the broadsheet affectation — the live terminal is the imagery and must stay central.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the output, don't describe it.** The real, structured CLI result is the hero. Evidence over adjectives.
2. **Warmth earns trust; the terminal proves competence.** A calm paper-and-ink page wrapping an honest dark terminal — the contrast is the brand.
3. **Determinism is the feeling.** Exact numbers, stable layout, no gratuitous motion. Nothing should feel guessed.
4. **Lowercase, mechanical voice.** The wordmark and wayfinding speak quietly and precisely, never shout.
5. **Restraint with a point of view.** One committed accent (clay), generous space, no decoration that isn't carrying meaning.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 in both light and dark themes (verify clay and muted tones against their real backgrounds). Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for every entrance/animation. Theme toggle (light/dark) and language toggle (EN/ES) are first-class and keyboard-operable. Terminal stays dark in both themes but must keep its own internal contrast.
